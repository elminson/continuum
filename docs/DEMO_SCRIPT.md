# Demo script (3:00)

Before recording: `php artisan continuum:demo --fresh`, `composer run dev`, open <http://localhost:8000> in a wide window, dark or light theme as you prefer. If an area has been backfilled with a MAP_KEY, use it for the calendar shots; otherwise Gran Chaco with the bundled sample works.

| Time      | Screen                                                                                           | What to say / do                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0:00–0:20 | **Overview** hero and KPI tiles                                                                  | "NASA has watched fires with two instruments for 26 years, but MODIS and VIIRS count the same fire differently. Across our sample VIIRS reports nine times as many pixels; after snapping both to one 1 km grid, six times as many cells remain — that residual is sensitivity, not splitting." Point at _Raw pixel inflation_ and _After 1 km gridding_. |
| 0:20–0:40 | Overview map (_Latest observed day_)                                                             | Toggle **Both → Modis → Viirs**. "Same day, same grid: the orange VIIRS surplus is exactly what a transfer function has to close." | 0:40–1:10 | **Gran Chaco** calendar page                                                                     | Open the area card. Tick **show the naive merge**: "This red line is what a raw archive says. Now the harmonized series:" untick it. Point at the KPI _Calibration −76% / −83% error_.                                         |
| 1:10–1:30 | Calendar heatmap + **Critical periods**                                                          | "Each cell is a month of harmonized activity; dashed cells are estimated from the other sensor. The season bar derives the climatological fire season, onset and peak, and places the latest month inside or outside it."                                                                                                                                 |
